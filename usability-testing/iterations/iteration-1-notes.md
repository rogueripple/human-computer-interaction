 Iteration 1 Notes

Baseline Prototype Review

The repository is associated with the Smart Cafeteria Human-Computer Interaction project. The initial usability review should concentrate on the application's information architecture and the main cafeteria user journey.

 Areas to Verify During Testing
- Discoverability of the main cafeteria/menu feature.
- Clarity of menu/item information.
- Ease of finding a specific meal or item.
- Clarity of the ordering flow.
- Visibility of order/status information.
- Consistency of navigation and back/home controls.
- Feedback after user actions.
- Error prevention and recovery.




After changes are made, repeat the affected task with participants and compare the result with the baseline.
