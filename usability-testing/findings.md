## Severity Guide
- **1 — Minor:** Small inconvenience; task remains easy.
- **2 — Moderate:** Noticeable confusion or extra effort, but task can normally be completed.
- **3 — Major:** Significant difficulty; users may need help or take a long time.
- **4 — Critical:** Prevents completion of an important task.
